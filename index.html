02 // Spatial Engineering

Bioclimatic Architectural Anatomy

Building in Accra requires acute environmental awareness. We do not rely on brute-force air conditioning alone. Our structures are passive climate machines designed to deflect harsh equatorial solar radiation while capturing coastal sea breezes.

01 // Solar Brise-Soleil Louvers Thermal Barrier

Precision-angled vertical native Iroko and teak louvers that shield east and west elevations, preventing peak heat gain while flooding interiors with soft, glare-free diffused daylight.

02 // Thermal Mass Concrete Core Passive Storage

High-density monolithic board-marked concrete acts as a thermal sponge, soaking up intense ambient daytime heat and radiating coolness into living zones.

03 // Aero-Stack Convection Voids Natural HVAC

Double-height interior lightwells create a natural chimney stack effect, exhausting warm rising air through concealed roof apertures and pulling cool ground-level breezes inside.

04 // Evaporative Perimeter Water Microclimate

Reflecting pools positioned along dominant southwest breeze vectors pre-cool incoming air by 3–4°C before it reaches interior living spaces.

03 // The Build Protocol

Why The Traditional Model Fails

Building in West Africa is notorious for stalled projects, finger-pointing between architects and rogue contractors, and escalating cost variations. Linework operates on a closed-loop system of single-point accountability.

The Broken Traditional Model

Fragmented & Adversarial

The typical pathway that causes 70% of Ghanaian projects to exceed budget or stall mid-construction:

✕
Paper-Only Architect: Sells beautiful 2D drawings without engineering cost-feasibility or material availability verification.
✕
Lowest-Bid Contractor: Promises unrealistic figures, cuts rebar grades, and brings unqualified site labor.
✕
The Blame Game: When structural cracks or rainwater leaks appear, the contractor blames the drawings, and the architect blames the pour.
✕
Client Trapped: 40%+ budget overruns, 9-month delivery delays, and emotional fatigue.
The Linework Integrated Model

Unified Turnkey Mastery

A single master entity responsible from the first pencil sketch to final key handover:

✓
Parametric 3D Pre-Construction: Every beam, conduit, and cantilever is verified in 3D BIM before site excavation begins.
✓
Direct General Contracting: Linework’s own certified structural engineers and master carpenters manage site execution.
✓
Single Throat to Choke: One contract. One unified team. Zero finger-pointing. Absolute accountability for every square millimeter.
✓
Fixed Milestone Schedule: Progress tied strictly to third-party lab concrete testing and milestone signoffs.

Building From Abroad Without Anxiety
